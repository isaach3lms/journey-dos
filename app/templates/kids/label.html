This screen did not offer that, and the reasoning was wrong in an instructive way. `window.print()` is genuinely absent from Apple's web view, so when check-in ran inside the installed app the button was dead and the fix was to open a PDF instead. But the lobby runs check-in in Safari, where `window.print()` has worked for years, and the PDF route costs the volunteer a new tab, a hidden toolbar, a share button they have to go looking for, and a second Print.
