{# The menu button. One button doing two jobs, chosen by width: - Wide: narrow the sidebar to its icons, and remember that. The choice is kept in localStorage rather than on the server, because it is about this screen rather than about this person: the same pastor wants the labels on a laptop and the icons on a 13 inch screen beside a plan. - Phone: slide the sidebar in over the page, with a scrim behind it. The drawer closes on the scrim, on Escape, and on following a link. It does not close on a resize into desktop width: the class it sets is scoped to the phone breakpoint in CSS, so a stale one is invisible. #}
