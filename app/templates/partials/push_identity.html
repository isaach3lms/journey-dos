{# Starting notifications, telling the app who is signed in, and reporting back what the phone said. The wrapper around this site loads the live pages rather than bundled files, so the web layer the provider's SDK expects to be started from is this one. That is the whole design: the wrapper's only job is to exist, and everything else is a deploy here rather than a rebuild, a resubmission and an App Store review. `initialize` once per app launch. Repeating it on every page would be harmless but pointless, and sessionStorage is exactly the lifetime of the app's web view. `login` on every page, because it is the one that can be wrong. A member who signs out and hands their phone to their spouse has to stop receiving the first person's notifications, and the wrapper has no idea either of those things happened. Setting it on every load means the id is always whoever is signed in now. **It no longer asks for permission on its own, and that is the point of this change.** iOS shows its prompt once per install, ever. Firing it the instant somebody signs in spends that single chance on a person who has not seen the app yet and does not know what they are being asked about, and a refusal is permanent short of a trip into iOS Settings. Asking is now a button on a screen that says why, and this file only hands the screens the means to ask. Nothing happens in an ordinary browser: there is no SDK there to call, the member app works normally, and the only thing missing is a notification a browser could not have shown anyway. #} {% if push_app_id %} {% if push_external_id %} {# The partial carries its own token rather than borrowing one from a form on the page.
